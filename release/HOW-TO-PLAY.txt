RUST & THUNDER
==============

A top-down pixel-art tank game with modern lighting, an arcade mode and a big campaign.

HOW TO START
  Double-click RustAndThunder.html. It opens in your web browser (Chrome, Edge or Firefox).
  Nothing to install, no internet needed. Press F for fullscreen.

TWO WAYS TO PLAY
  1 PLAYER / 2 PLAYERS  The classic arcade game: protect the eagle, destroy every tank on the stage.
  CAMPAIGN              Drive your scrappy captured tank BABUSYA across five big regions.
                        Free villages, salvage wrecks, upgrade the tank in the workshop,
                        recruit a crew and take down each region's boss.
                        The campaign saves itself in your browser (CONTINUE on the menu).

CONTROLS (keyboard)
  Move ............ Arrow keys or WASD
  Fire ............ Space
  Pause ........... Esc or P
  Campaign:
    1 ............. FPV drone (hold to aim, release to launch, tap for a quick strike)
    2 ............. Infantry ambush (place in trees or ruins)
    3 ............. Recon drone (shows hidden enemies and mines)
    4 ............. Patch armour
    Tab ........... Big map
    Enter ......... Workshop, when parked at a freed village's flag

  Two players on one keyboard:
    Player 1 ...... WASD to move, Space to fire, 1-4 abilities, H for the workshop
    Player 2 ...... Arrow keys to move, Enter to fire, Numpad 1-4 abilities

  Gamepads work too (hold LB or RB + d-pad for abilities, Back for the map).

OTHER KEYS
  F fullscreen   M mute   R lighting on/off   C retro CRT look   ` show frame rate

TIPS
  - Your tank can take about 4 hits in the campaign. Press 4 to patch it (once every 15 seconds).
  - Wrecks drop scrap and parts: drive over them.
  - Bosses flash and glow before their big shots: keep moving.
  - Losing all your lives is a retreat, not game over: you keep your progress and try again a little easier.
  - If the game runs slowly on your computer it lowers its own resolution to stay smooth.
